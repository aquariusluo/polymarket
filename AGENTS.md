5. `bankroll_usd` — total capital reserved for this strategy.
6. `max_copy_usd_per_order` — hard cap per copied trade.
7. `max_daily_orders` — hard cap on copied trades per day.
8. `slippage_bps` — maximum tolerated slippage before rejecting or flagging a trade.
9. `evaluation_start_date` — starting date for the evaluation window.
10. `evaluation_days` — number of days before writing the formal review verdict.
11. `excluded_wallets` — wallets that must be ignored even if they rank highly.
